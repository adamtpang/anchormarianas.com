# Guam ICP index: teaching AI and implementing for a price

Different question than the outreach ranking. Not who is easiest to reach, but who has AI-absorbable work, can pay for it, decides fast, and lets one build be resold across the vertical.

Score = (absorb x3 + pay x3 + decide x2 + reuse x2) scaled by how many reachable leads exist. Judgment inputs 1 to 5, reasoning on every row.

## Why each ranks where it does
- **Professional Services** (46): Law, accounting, insurance, real estate. Intake, drafting, quotes, document Q and A. Staff are knowledge workers who will sit through training.
- **Trades & Home Services** (44): Missed calls are lost jobs. Owner answers the phone and decides on the spot. Wants it done, not taught.
- **Dental** (42): Booking, reminders, recalls, no-show chasing, insurance FAQ. Near-identical across every practice.
- **Beauty & Salons** (38): Booking friction and unanswered reviews are visible from outside. Small budgets, fast decisions.
- **Healthcare** (33): Same admin load as dental, bigger budgets, but clinics have more approval layers.
- **Food & Drink** (31): Reviews and order questions. Thin margins, high volume, mostly a low-ticket recurring play.
- **Other** (28): Mixed bag, not a coherent ICP.
- **Auto** (27): Quotes and service scheduling. Middle on every axis.
- **Hospitality & Lodging** (26): Concierge, booking, multilingual guest questions. Highest value per deal, slowest to reach and approve.
- **Retail** (26): Inventory and hours questions. Least AI-absorbable of the reachable groups.
- **Tourism & Events** (24): Itinerary questions and multilingual inquiries. Seasonal budgets.
- **Fitness & Leisure** (23): Membership questions and scheduling. Low ticket.
- **Veterinary & Pet** (23): Same shape as dental, but only a handful of practices on island.
- **Education & Childcare** (15): Enrollment questions. Tiny sample in the data.
- **Fuel** (9): Almost no addressable knowledge work. Corporate-owned.

## How to read it
- **absorb**: share of their daily work that is repetitive language work AI can take.
- **pay**: ability to pay a real implementation price, not just a $150/mo add-on.
- **decide**: owner decides on the spot (5) vs layers of approval (1).
- **reuse**: one build resells across the vertical with light edits. This is the compounding axis.
- **sell as**: verticals whose staff do knowledge work will buy training. Trades and salons want it done for them, not taught.
