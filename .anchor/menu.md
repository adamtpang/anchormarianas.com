Status: ✅ = live one-click checkout (Stripe) · 📞 = book a call to start (invoice or a link I mint on the spot)

## How the menu sells itself
1. Free scan surfaces the problems (from real Google Maps + reviews data).
2. Each problem is already tagged to a menu item and a price above.
3. The $1,500 audit quantifies and sequences them.
4. They pick off the menu. No custom quotes, no scoping drag.

## Notes to self
- The deep audit (presence + reviews + stack layers) should auto-tag which menu items each business needs.
- The 📞 items are one `/mint` away from ✅ live checkout whenever you want them buyable without a call (the $1,500 audit took 60 seconds).
- Lead with the outcome line (bold), never the tool name. "Stop losing missed-call customers" sells; "AI receptionist" does not.
- Keep prices honest and fixed. A menu with quiet exceptions is not a menu.
