# Anchor Marianas — Business Brief
_Last updated: 2026-07-09_

## What Anchor is (one sentence)
An **AI consultancy** that finds where AI saves a business time and money, then builds it. Productized, practitioner-delivered, starting in Guam.

Model: Martell's productized consulting. A free diagnostic that converts into a paid engagement ("$5k audit becomes a $50k implementation"). Free scan -> $1,500 audit -> the build.

## The three verbs
- **Diagnose** — the free AnchorScan and the paid audit find where AI helps.
- **Cure** — Adam builds the fix (AI receptionist, review responder, websites, custom builds). This is where the money is.
- **Educate** — the scan and audit teach owners what AI can do. The ICP knows they should use AI but not where to start, so whoever shows them first, credibly, wins.

## What Anchor is NOT
- NOT a SaaS or a tools company. The scan, audit, menu, /build, and the Apify scraper are funnel and tooling, not products for sale. Roadmap says productize LAST.
- NOT a slide-deck consultancy. Anchor diagnoses AND delivers, because Adam actually ships. That is the moat.

## The operating model (the assembly line)
1. **List** — all Guam businesses as one leads list (Apify Google Maps sweep).
2. **Score and rank** — the deep-audit engine tags each business's problems and prices them, then ranks the list so we work it in the right order.
3. **Diagnose (free)** — a personalized read from their own reviews (the DM / the scan).
4. **Price (the menu)** — every detected problem already has a fixed price. No custom quotes.
5. **Cure (paid)** — Adam builds the solution.
6. **Upsell** — one cure leads to the next (care plan, more automations, recurring).

## The "right order" (prioritization)
Rank each lead by: **opportunity dollars (sum of detected problems' prices) x reachability (direct phone/WhatsApp > DM > walk-in) x pain obviousness (undeniable from their own reviews).** Work highest first.
