# Call script: HAPI + ICON Builders, vendor-side problem interviews

Prepped 2026-08-11 for the Guam senior-housing market-qualification thesis
(`../../../ObsidianVault/projects/silver-generation-guam-ledger.md`, Step 4 of
the 7-day test). Purpose: learn real operational pain points from two Hawaii
vendors comparable to Guma' Trankilidat's needs: a HUD/LIHTC/USDA RD 515
administrator and an occupied affordable-housing renovation contractor. This
is a discovery interview, not a hire pitch or a sale.

## What's confirmed before dialing

- **Hawaii Affordable Properties, Inc. (HAPI)**: ~30 years administering HUD
  Section 202, Section 8, LIHTC, and USDA Rural Development Section 515 (the
  exact program that financed Guma' Trankilidat). Manages 4,000+ units across
  33 properties statewide. The standout comparable on the full list of ten
  Hawaii-based delivery firms named 2026-08-05.
- **ICON Builders**: specializes specifically in occupied affordable-housing
  renovations, 30+ years, LIHTC/HUD/bond-funded projects. Closest match to
  what Genesis Tech Corp and Pacific-Green Integrated Technology were hired to
  do at Guma' Trankilidat, done at Hawaii scale.
- Neither firm is confirmed to already have a Guam presence: absence of
  evidence from the 2026-08-05 search, not a verified negative. Worth asking
  directly.
- Per the arena map, permitted introductions are Adam's call to make: this is
  intelligence-gathering on the market, not a cold sales pitch.

## The call

**Opening (say this, don't wing it):**

> "Hi, I'm researching the affordable senior-housing services market and
> drawing comparisons to a HUD/LIHTC project in Guam called Guma'
> Trankilidat. Would you have 10-15 minutes to talk through your experience?
> I'm not selling anything, just trying to understand the real operational
> picture."

**HAPI: questions, in priority order:**

1. What's the hardest part of managing compliance across that many HUD/LIHTC
   properties at once?
2. Where do things actually go wrong most often: reporting, occupancy,
   maintenance backlog, funding renewal?
3. Have you ever looked at island or Pacific expansion, Guam specifically?
   What would stop you?
4. If you were solving your single biggest unmet need today, what would you
   build or fix first?

**ICON Builders: questions, in priority order:**

1. What's uniquely hard about renovating occupied affordable housing versus
   vacant units?
2. How do you handle LIHTC/HUD/bond compliance mid-renovation?
3. Any island or remote-location projects? What logistics broke that you
   didn't expect?
4. What's your biggest recurring friction working with owners/administrators
   like HAPI?

**Close, either call:** ask directly whether they'd take a follow-up call if
a real Guam opportunity firms up. That's the actual ask: not a hire, not a
sale, just keeping the door open.
