# OFFER: AnchorScan to implementation

Updated 2026-08-24.

## One-liner

For Guam business owners who know something is costing them customers but do
not know which problem to fix first, Anchor turns their public site, listing,
and reviews into an evidence-backed report, then implements the right fix at a
clear price.

## Front door

- Instant AnchorScan: free, no signup, website-only diagnostic.
- Deep Anchor report: currently free while the report workflow is being proven.
- Review Responder pilot: free for the first 10 Guam businesses, then $150 per
  month. The pilot starts only after the scan finds unanswered reviews and the
  business agrees that the problem matters.

## What the report contains

For each finding:

1. The exact source and observed problem.
2. The question that still needs an operator answer.
3. A solution Anchor can actually deliver.
4. The implementation scope and limits.
5. A current market-rate range with dated links.
6. Anchor's fixed price, or `research required` if it is not ready to quote.

## Price ladder

The live catalogue is in `content/services.json` and `/pricing`. Current anchor
points include $497 for a five-day landing page, $997 for selected audits or
performance work, $1,500 for an AI Opportunity Audit, $1,997 for a five-day
MVP, and recurring work from $150 to $4,950 per month.

These are offer prices, not claims about universal market value. Re-check live
market sources before raising or defending a price.

## Risk reversal

- Scan first, sell second.
- No invented numbers or fabricated review findings.
- Fixed scope and price before paid implementation begins.
- The first 10 Review Responder pilots require no card or contract. The
  business gives direct feedback and can stop the pilot.
- The five-day build guarantee remains governed by the public terms.
