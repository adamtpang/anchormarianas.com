Adam is the board and final owner. One root agent coordinates work. The eight
operating departments are fixed:

| Department | Owns current scan-first workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering | Scan, report generation, data sources, deployment, reliability |
| Design | Report readability, site experience, accessibility |
| Marketing | Positioning and public proof after evidence exists |
| Sales | Authorized report delivery, follow-up, qualification, closing |
| Finance | Market-rate research, Anchor pricing, receipts, runway |
| Operations | Source freshness, report QA, evidence log, process |

| Legal | Terms, privacy, customer data, claims, outbound compliance |

## One constraint

The company has one current binding constraint: no verified end-to-end report
has completed the scan, problem, solution, market-rate, and human-QA sequence.
Engineering owns the report path, Operations owns QA, and Finance owns the
market-rate evidence. Sales remains gated until Adam authorizes outbound.

Owners return `SOLVED`, `BLOCKED`, or `STOPPED` with evidence. Activity without
an inspectable artifact or changed outcome is not completion.
