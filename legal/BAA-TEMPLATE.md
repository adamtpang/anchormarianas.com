This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] between [PRACTICE LEGAL NAME], located at [PRACTICE ADDRESS] ("Covered Entity"), and Anchor Marianas, LLC, located at [ANCHOR ADDRESS] ("Business Associate").

## 1. Purpose

Business Associate provides missed-call text-back, after-hours call answering, and related patient-communication services to Covered Entity under [SERVICE AGREEMENT OR ORDER FORM, DATE] (the "Services Agreement"). In doing so, Business Associate may create, receive, maintain, or transmit Protected Health Information on behalf of Covered Entity. This Agreement sets the terms under which it may do so.

## 2. Definitions

Terms used but not defined here have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules, 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, including: Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices, Protected Health Information ("PHI"), Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and Use. PHI here means only PHI that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity.

## 3. Obligations of Business Associate

Business Associate agrees to:

a. Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement or as Required by Law.

b. Use appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 (the Security Rule) with respect to electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided by this Agreement.

c. Report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement of which it becomes aware, including Breaches of Unsecured PHI as required by 45 CFR 164.410, and any Security Incident of which it becomes aware. Business Associate will report without unreasonable delay and no later than [10] calendar days after discovery, and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovery of a Breach. The report will include, to the extent available, the identity of each affected Individual and the information Covered Entity needs to meet its own notification duties. The parties agree that unsuccessful Security Incidents, such as pings, port scans, and blocked log-in attempts that do not result in unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, need no individual report; this sentence serves as notice of their ongoing occurrence.

d. Under 45 CFR 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements that apply to Business Associate. Business Associate's current Subcontractors are listed in Exhibit A, and Business Associate will update Exhibit A before adding one.

e. Make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity within [15] days of request, so Covered Entity can meet 45 CFR 164.524. Business Associate is not expected to maintain a Designated Record Set unless the Services Agreement says so.

f. Make amendments to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed by Covered Entity under 45 CFR 164.526, within [15] days of request.

g. Maintain and provide, within [15] days of request, the information required for Covered Entity to give an accounting of disclosures under 45 CFR 164.528.

h. To the extent Business Associate carries out any of Covered Entity's obligations under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164, comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in performing that obligation.

i. Make its internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

j. Forward to Covered Entity, within [5] business days, any request an Individual makes directly to Business Associate for access, amendment, or an accounting.

k. Request, use, and disclose only the Minimum Necessary PHI to perform the Services.

l. Not use PHI to train, fine-tune, or improve any artificial-intelligence model, and not sell PHI.

## 4. Permitted uses and disclosures by Business Associate

a. Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Services described in the Services Agreement.

b. Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as Required by Law.

c. Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities. It may disclose PHI for those purposes only if the disclosure is Required by Law, or if Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurance from the recipient that the PHI will be held confidentially, used or further disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Business Associate of any known breach of confidentiality.

d. Business Associate will not provide Data Aggregation services, and will not de-identify PHI, unless the Services Agreement expressly permits it.

e. Business Associate will not use or disclose PHI in any way that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted in 4(c) and 4(d).

## 5. Obligations of Covered Entity

Covered Entity will:

a. Notify Business Associate of any limitation in its Notice of Privacy Practices that may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

b. Notify Business Associate of any change in, or revocation of, an Individual's permission to use or disclose PHI, to the extent it may affect Business Associate.

c. Notify Business Associate of any restriction on use or disclosure of PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to under 45 CFR 164.522, to the extent it may affect Business Associate.

d. Not ask Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

e. Be responsible for obtaining any patient consent needed for text messages sent at Covered Entity's direction, including consent required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, unless the Services Agreement assigns that task to Business Associate.

## 6. Term and termination

a. **Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and ends when the Services Agreement ends, unless terminated earlier under this section.

b. **Termination for cause.** If either party determines that the other has violated a material term of this Agreement, it will give written notice and an opportunity to cure within [30] days. If the violation is not cured, or cure is not possible, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and the Services Agreement.

c. **Obligations on termination.** Upon termination, Business Associate will return to Covered Entity or destroy all PHI it still maintains in any form, including PHI held by its Subcontractors, and retain no copies, within [30] days. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate will notify Covered Entity of the conditions that make it infeasible, extend the protections of this Agreement to that PHI, and limit further uses and disclosures to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for as long as it maintains the PHI. Where a Subcontractor's standard retention period applies (listed in Exhibit A), Business Associate will ensure the PHI is deleted at the end of that period.

d. **Survival.** Section 6(c) survives termination.

## 7. Miscellaneous

a. **Regulatory references.** A reference to a section of the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended.

b. **Amendment.** The parties will amend this Agreement as needed to keep it consistent with changes in the HIPAA Rules.

c. **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity will be resolved to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules. If this Agreement conflicts with the Services Agreement on the handling of PHI, this Agreement controls.

d. **No third-party beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Agreement gives any person other than the parties any rights.

e. **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Guam, except where federal law controls.
