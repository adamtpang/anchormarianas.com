# Market benchmark: what AI consultancies actually charge (2026)

Provenance: web research 2026-07-25, sources linked below. Figures are published market rates from other firms, not Anchor's results. Confirm before quoting to a client.

## The headline: Anchor is priced at roughly a tenth of the market

| Tier | Market rate (2026) | Anchor today | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI readiness assessment / audit | $2,000 to $8,000 | $1,500 | below the market floor |
| Quick-win project | ~$5,000 | $497 to $1,997 | 3x to 10x under |
| Full implementation | $15,000 to $50,000 | nothing offered | missing tier |
| Ongoing partnership | $4,500/mo (SMB tier) | $500/mo care | 9x under |
| Hourly (independent to boutique) | $150 to $1,000/hr | n/a, fixed price only | fixed price is the right call |

Read carefully: being cheap is not automatically wrong. It buys speed to first client and it fits Guam SMB budgets, which are smaller than mainland SMB budgets. But two things are true at once. The $1,500 audit sits UNDER the market floor for the same deliverable, and there is no tier above $1,997, so a client who wants a real build has nothing to buy.

## The missing product: paid teaching

Anchor sells diagnose and cure. The market also sells EDUCATE, and it is priced well:

| Format | Market rate |
|---|---|
| Half-day team workshop | ~$999 |
| Full-day team workshop (up to 5 people) | ~$1,499 |
| Executive briefing (90 min, larger group) | up to $7,500 |
| Ongoing per-seat working group | $150 to $300 per person per month |
| Team training programs | $1,500 to $3,000/mo |

The pattern worth stealing: **many providers credit the workshop fee toward a larger engagement.** That is exactly Anchor's audit-credits-toward-the-build mechanic, applied one rung earlier. A workshop is a cheap, low-risk first yes that pays for itself and pre-sells the implementation.

## Why this matters right now

The Guam ICP index ranks Professional Services (#1), Dental (#3), and Healthcare (#5) as the top ICPs, and all three are the verticals whose staff do knowledge work, meaning they will buy TRAINING, not just done-for-you automation. Anchor's top-ranked ICPs want a product Anchor does not currently sell.

## Peer firms already studied (see anchor_guam_ai_playbook memory)

- **Smith.ai**: one wedge service sold narrow (AI receptionist), $10M+ ARR.
- **Vendasta**: diagnose-first, snapshot report then sell the gaps. Anchor's AnchorScan is the same move.
- **Left Click**: niche-or-die, dominate one vertical then ride referrals.
- **Morningside**: productized fixed prices, no custom quotes.
- **Chris Kerner**: free asset as the foot in the door, upsell recurring care.

Every one of them, at Anchor's stage, grew the same way: founder-led volume outreach to the first three clients, then case study, then referrals.
