## Line 1: AI services for Marianas SMBs

🎯 **THESIS**: Bring practical AI automation to Marianas SMBs so they aren't
left behind by the AI wave. Local-first, services-led now, productizing into
recurring SaaS once a wedge proves itself.

⚙️ **MECHANISM**
1. Anchor Scan: free AI-generated diagnostic report from a business's Google
   reviews. Demand-led: observations + questions, no invented numbers, no
   prescription.
2. The diagnostic surfaces real patterns → a warm 15-min discovery call.
3. Fixed-price builds close the first transaction: $497 landing page /
   $1,997 MVP, 5 days or refunded.
4. A $500/mo care plan converts one-off builds into recurring revenue.
5. The IDI case study (real, named, live, free AI-automation work on IDI's
   own internal operations, family business, unpaid, approved by a real
   decision-maker there as a deliberate proof-of-value case study, not a
   retainer or a discount on the menu above) is the proof-of-work anchor
   for credibility. Once real time/money/revenue impact is proven there,
   it becomes the pitch for paid Fractional AI Engineer work at comparable
   Guam wholesalers/distributors. See idiguam.com's repos.yaml for the
   live status of that work.

💰 **MODEL**: Near-term: project-based pilots ($497–$1,997 fixed builds) +
a $500/mo retainer. Long-term: recurring SaaS once Anchor Scan proves a
repeatable pattern across enough discovery calls.

📍 **Where this stands (2026-08-09)**: Live and shipping. Site UX/legal/SEO
pass complete: refund guarantee consistent everywhere a price appears,
privacy/terms live, careers and /guam pages shipped, sitemap complete,
Anchor Scan reads published for 5 businesses. Branch hygiene clean (just
`main` + active work).

## Line 2: Silver-generation rollup (Guam eldercare acquisition)

🎯 **THESIS**: Acquire a non-medical home-care business on Guam, SBA
7(a)-financed, seller-financed where possible, and run it with an
AI-automated back office. A search-fund-style first acquisition, not a
rollup on day one: prove one deal operationally before a second.

⚙️ **MECHANISM**
1. Source real, named, licensed non-medical home-care operators on Guam
   (current list: Live Well Home Care of Guam, Guam Home Care; Care Connect
   LLC unverified; Guam Visiting Nurses likely disqualified: see
   decisions-log.md correction round).
2. Build relationships before any acquisition ask: learn the market from
   operators first, per Brad Jacobs' own method (patient, pleasant, polite,
   never play hard to get).
3. Solve financing: Bank of Guam / Coast360 / BankPacific all theoretically
   capable by balance sheet ($3.04B, $370M+ respectively); real unknown is
   underwriting appetite, testable only by an actual application.
4. Close deal #1, standardize (one platform, one KPI dashboard), prove the
   model before deal #2.

💰 **MODEL**: SBA 7(a) primary leverage (up to ~90% of deal value under
$5M) + seller note + 10-20% buyer equity injection, personally guaranteed.
Target class: non-medical home care specifically: SBA does not finance
Medicare-certified home health/hospice, which rules out a large share of
Guam's eldercare-adjacent operators.

📍 **Where this stands (2026-08-09)**: Guam-first (overrode the original
Hawaii-first call). 3 Firecrawl sourcing monitors live on Guam listings.
Target list narrowing, not closed. No financing conversation started yet.
Visiting Angels Hawaii call script shelved as fallback, not deleted.
